Doorbell Face privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Doorbell Face is a personal project used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What data it accesses

  • Events and short video clip previews from the owner's own Google Nest doorbell, via the Google Smart Device Management API.
  • Face embeddings (numeric face descriptors) of household members, created by the owner from their own photos.

How the data is used

Clips are analysed to recognise household members, and an alert with a snapshot is sent to the owner's own WhatsApp number. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

All data stays in the owner's private Google Cloud project. Event records are deleted automatically after 7 days. Google user data is used in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
